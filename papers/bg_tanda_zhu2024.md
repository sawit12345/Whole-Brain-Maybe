## Key points (from fetched abstract page + search snippets)
- Cortico-basal-ganglia computational model including tonically active
  neurons (TANs, striatal cholinergic interneurons) reciprocally interacting
  with dopamine (DA).
- Pathway semantics in default state: direct pathway promotes,
  indirect pathway suppresses, hyperdirect pathway stops formation of
  stimulus-action associations.
- Reinforcement: a single rewarded action is selected through a combined
  TAN-DA-dependent reinforcement mechanism plus Hebbian mechanism, with a
  gradual transfer from reinforcement-driven to Hebbian (habit) control.
- Switching reward to a new action yields a longer exploratory phase because
  prior Hebbian habituation must be overcome.
- Parkinsonian state: reinforcement mechanism disrupted, dopamine deficiency;
  levodopa improves but does not fully restore performance; explained via TAN
  pause duration and phasic DA release magnitude.
